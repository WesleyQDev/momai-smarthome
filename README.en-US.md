# MomAI Smart Home

Control the lights, air conditioning, TV, and outlets in your home through MomAI, by voice or text. The extension talks to the Home Assistant setup you already use.

## What you can do

- Turn lamps on and off, change their color, and adjust brightness.
- Set the air conditioning temperature and check how each room feels.
- Turn the TV on or off, change the volume, and control playback.
- See devices grouped by room in the sidebar.
- Open an on-screen control to fine-tune a lamp or the TV.
- Keep control on your local network.

## Examples

- "Turn on the living room light."
- "Set the office lamp to blue at 50% brightness."
- "Set the bedroom air conditioning to 22 degrees."
- "Turn off the living room TV."
- "Turn off all the lights."

## How to set up

1. In Home Assistant, open your profile and create a Long-Lived Access Token.
2. Copy the address you use to open Home Assistant in the browser. For example: http://homeassistant.local:8123.
3. In MomAI, open Smart Home in the sidebar.
4. Paste the address and the token.
5. Click Connect. MomAI finds the devices registered in your home.

## Permissions

- **Network:** to talk to your Home Assistant on the local network.
