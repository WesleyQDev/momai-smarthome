# MomAI Smart Home

The MomAI Smart Home extension turns your computer into a true smart home command center, allowing you to control lights, air conditioning, TVs, and outlets using natural voice or text commands.

## Your connected home, under your control

Integrated with Home Assistant, MomAI can communicate directly with the devices installed in your home, offering both quick chat responses and a full visual panel with dedicated control screens.

### Key Features

- **Lighting Control**: Turn on, turn off, change lamp colors, and adjust brightness in every room of your home.
- **Climate & Comfort**: Adjust air conditioning temperature and monitor room thermal comfort.
- **TVs & Audio**: Turn the living room TV on or off, change volume, and control media playback without leaving your computer.
- **Visual Panel by Rooms**: View the state of all devices organized by rooms (such as Living Room, Bedroom, Kitchen, or Office) in the sidebar.
- **Interactive Floating Windows**: When you ask to adjust a lamp or TV, MomAI can open a visual remote control or color wheel on your screen for fine adjustments.
- **100% Local Communication**: Control happens within your local network, ensuring fast responses and complete privacy.

## What you can ask MomAI

- "MomAI, turn on the living room light."
- "Change the office lamp to blue at 50% brightness."
- "Set the bedroom air conditioning to 22 degrees."
- "Turn off the living room TV."
- "Turn off all the lights in the house."

## How to Set Up

1. In your Home Assistant, go to your user profile settings and generate a Long-Lived Access Token.
2. Copy the address you use to access Home Assistant in your browser (for example: http://homeassistant.local:8123).
3. Open MomAI and click the Smart Home icon in the sidebar.
4. Paste the server address and token code in the configuration fields.
5. Click Connect. The assistant will automatically identify all devices registered in your home.
